## 1. Commitment to Copyright Protection

Setsuzoku Media Labs respects the intellectual property rights of creators, studios, musicians, and rights-holders. We maintain a prompt notice-and-takedown procedure to address claims of alleged copyright infringement on Setsuzoku OS.

---

## 2. Submitting an Infringement Takedown Notice

If you believe that your copyrighted work has been copied, displayed, or hosted on Setsuzoku OS without authorization, you (or your authorized agent) must provide a written notice containing the following details:

1. **Identification of the Protected Work:** A clear description of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (or a link/registration certificate).
2. **Identification of the Infringing Material:** Specific details to help us locate the infringing content on Setsuzoku OS (e.g. project URL, deliverable version ID, file name, or client portal link).
3. **Contact Information:** Your full legal name, company name (if applicable), physical address, telephone number, and email address.
4. **Good Faith Statement:** A statement that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law (such as fair dealing or fair use).
5. **Accuracy & Authority Statement:** A statement under penalty of perjury that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
6. **Physical or Electronic Signature:** A physical or verified electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.

Please deliver the complete notice to:
**Designated Copyright Agent**  
Setsuzoku Media Labs Private Limited  
Email: **info@setsuzoku.in**  
Subject Line: `COPYRIGHT INFRINGEMENT NOTICE - [Your Name / Company]`  

---

## 3. Our Response & Takedown Action

Upon receiving a valid and complete infringement notice:
1. We will expeditiously review the claim within 36 business hours.
2. If validated, we will disable access to or remove the disputed deliverable or file.
3. We will immediately notify the affected user or client who uploaded the content, providing them with a copy of your notice so they may respond with a counter-notice if they believe the removal was made in error.

---

## 4. Counter-Notice Procedure (For Content Uploaders)

If you believe that material you uploaded was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification, you may submit a formal Counter-Notice to our Designated Agent containing:

1. Identification of the specific material that was removed or disabled and the location where it appeared before removal.
2. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake, misidentification, or licensed fair dealing.
3. Your full legal name, address, telephone number, and email address.
4. Consent to the jurisdiction of the competent courts in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.
5. Your physical or electronic signature.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notice, Setsuzoku will forward a copy to the original complaining party. Unless the complaining party initiates formal legal action seeking a court order within 14 business days, Setsuzoku may restore the removed material.

---

## 5. Repeat Infringer Policy

Setsuzoku maintains a strict policy to terminate accounts of users or organizations determined to be repeat copyright infringers.
