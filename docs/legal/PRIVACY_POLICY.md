## 1. Scope & Commitment to Privacy

Setsuzoku respects the fundamental privacy rights of all Data Principals. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is safeguarded, and how you may exercise your statutory rights under the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023**.

---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect (Data Minimization)

We collect only the personal data strictly required to deliver agency operating system services:
- **Identity & Contact Data:** Name, business email address, corporate phone number, job title.
- **Organization & Billing Data:** Company legal name, registered billing address, GST identification number (GSTIN), primary currency.
- **Client & Lead Contact Records:** Customer names, official client emails, designated decision-makers entered by agency managers.
- **Technical & Audit Logs:** Session IP addresses, user-agent metadata, and audit event logs recorded for financial integrity and multi-tenant security verification.

We do **not** collect sensitive biometric data, health data, or political affiliations.

---

## 3. Lawful Grounds for Processing

We process personal data solely on the basis of:
1. **Explicit Consent:** Freely given, specific, informed, and unconditional consent obtained during registration or public lead intake.
2. **Performance of a Contract:** Fulfilling production milestones, deliverable sign-offs, and commercial quotations.
3. **Legal & Regulatory Obligations:** Compliance with Indian taxation (GST records retention) and cyber security incident reporting rules (CERT-In).

---

## 4. Zero Data Selling & Zero AI Model Training

- **No Data Selling:** We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or client directories to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.
- **No Generative AI Training:** We do not train, fine-tune, or supply your project information, media assets, or client emails to any machine learning models.

---

## 5. Security & Storage Safeguards

1. **Encryption in Transit:** All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is protected using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
2. **Encryption at Rest:** Sensitive identifiers and databases are maintained behind encrypted storage volumes.
3. **Server-Side Isolation:** Multi-tenant separation ensures no organization can access or query another organization's records.
4. **Daily Encrypted Backups:** Automated daily snapshots stored in isolated recovery vaults with documented restore verification.

---

## 6. Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP Act 2023)

Under Section 11, 12, and 13 of the DPDP Act 2023, you have the following rights:
1. **Right to Access Information:** You may review a summary of your personal data being processed.
2. **Right to Data Portability / Export:** Accessible via `GET /api/v1/privacy/export` to download a machine-readable JSON archive of your personal records.
3. **Right to Correction & Erasure:** Accessible via `POST /api/v1/privacy/delete-request` to request anonymization or erasure of your personal data where retention is no longer mandated by tax legislation.
4. **Right to Grievance Redressal:** You have the right to register concerns with our Data Protection Officer.
5. **Right to Nominate:** You may nominate an individual to exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or incapacity.

---

## 7. Data Retention Schedule

- **Active Accounts:** Retained for the duration of your active platform engagement.
- **Tax & Invoicing Ledgers:** Retained for statutory 7-year periods in accordance with Indian GST and corporate auditing rules.
- **Erased / Withdrawn Accounts:** Personal contact fields are scrubbed and permanently anonymized within 30 days of a verified erasure request.
