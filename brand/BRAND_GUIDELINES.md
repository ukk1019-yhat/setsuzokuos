## 3. Strict Non-Alteration Rules

To preserve brand identity and avoid trademark dilution, the brand mark must never be altered in UI components or collateral:

1. **No Distortion or Aspect Ratio Changes:** The aspect ratio of the logomark and figures must always be locked at 1:1. Never stretch or condense the mark.
2. **No Color Alterations:** The primary figures (cyan/blue and emerald/teal) and the central accent must not be recolored, duotoned, or filtered.
3. **No Decorative Gradients or AI Effects:** Never apply generic AI glow effects, neon borders, drop shadows with color bleeds, or glassmorphism blurs over the logo.
4. **Minimum Clear Space:** Maintain a clear margin of at least 25% of the mark's height around all four sides free from text, icons, or visual noise.
5. **Minimum Size:**
   - Digital Favicon / Compact Mark: Minimum 24px $\times$ 24px.
   - Sidebar Navigation Lockup: Minimum 28px mark height.
   - Document / PDF Export Header: Minimum 32px height.
